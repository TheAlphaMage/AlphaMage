## `I` — About

> I take systems apart until the mechanism becomes visible, then rebuild them into
> something that works. My work sits where **applied mathematics, deep learning and
> low-level engineering** meet — from deriving a gradient by hand to shipping the
> container that runs it.

I care about the layer where a formula stops being notation and starts being
behaviour. That means I work comfortably at both ends of the stack: proofs,
tensors and loss landscapes on one side; memory, concurrency and kernel-level
reality on the other.

- 🔭 **Building** agentic systems, autonomous workflows and evaluation harnesses.
- 🧠 **Studying** deep learning internals, representation learning and emergence.
- ∴ **Deriving** before implementing — backprop, optimisation, information theory.
- ⚙ **Engineering** in C, modern C++ and Rust, on Red Hat Linux.
- ♟ **Playing** competitive chess — pattern recognition under a clock.
